# Instrument sample attribution

MUSICINATE includes selected MP3 notes from the FluidR3 GM SoundFont by Frank Wen. The MIDI.js Soundfonts project rendered and encoded these samples. MUSICINATE selects root notes and changes their pitch, gain, and envelopes at playback.

- [MIDI.js Soundfonts — FluidR3 GM and license](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts/tree/gh-pages#soundfonts-available)
- [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 United States](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/us/)

## Drum samples

The drum one-shots were rendered with FluidSynth from the FluidR3 GM standard percussion kit distributed in Debian's [fluid-soundfont-gm package](https://packages.debian.org/fluid-soundfont-gm). MUSICINATE trims and encodes the rendered hits.
